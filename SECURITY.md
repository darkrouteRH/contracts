# Security

If you find something, tell us in private first. We would rather owe you than read about it.

**Where to send it**

- Email: support@darkroute.exchange
- GitHub: [private security advisory](https://github.com/darkrouteRH/extension/security/advisories/new)
  (that repository is the mailbox for every DarkRoute project, including this one)
- X: [@DarkrouteRH](https://x.com/DarkrouteRH) for a first contact

Machine readable: [darkroute.exchange/.well-known/security.txt](https://darkroute.exchange/.well-known/security.txt)

**What we do**

Acknowledge within 3 days, tell you what we found when we reproduce it, fix and deploy, and credit
you publicly if you want that. Scope, the severity ladder and the rules are at
[darkroute.exchange/security](https://darkroute.exchange/security).

**What we pay**

Rewards are sized to impact and to what a router with almost no revenue can afford. Every paid
report is published in full, with the amount and the transaction that paid it, at
[darkroute.exchange/security/reports](https://darkroute.exchange/security/reports), including the
parts that stayed open.

**Please**

Report privately, do not touch other people's data to prove a point, and give us ninety days from
acknowledgement or until the fix ships, whichever comes first.
